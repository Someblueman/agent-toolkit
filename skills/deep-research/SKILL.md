# Deep Research

Placeholder instructions. Real guidance belongs here.

## When to load

- User asks a "what is the best way to ..." or "compare X vs Y" question.
- The answer likely spans multiple authoritative sources.

## Behavior

1. Decompose the question into 3-5 sub-questions.
2. For each, identify the most authoritative source class (docs, primary source, expert).
3. Synthesize, then surface disagreements rather than hiding them.
